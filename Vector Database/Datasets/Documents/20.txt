2024-2025 ACADEMIC YEAR SPRING SEMESTER TUITION FEES AND PAYMENT METHODS

Dear Students,

It was previously announced that tuition fee payments can be made either:
- As a single annual payment (covering both Fall and Spring semesters) during the Fall registration period, OR
- As separate semester payments during Fall and Spring registration periods.

If semester payments are chosen, the Spring semester tuition fee may increase, limited to the June-November inflation rate.

Below are the tuition fees and payment methods for students continuing in Undergraduate Programs and the Foundation Development Year in the 2024-2025 Academic Year Spring semester.

According to Sabancı University Undergraduate Education and Teaching Regulations, Article 13-1:
Students are required to pay their semester tuition fees, complete course registrations, and renew their semester registrations at the beginning of each semester within the announced academic calendar periods. Late payments will result in delayed registration renewal and late payment interest for lump-sum payments.

Payment Options:
- Payments can be made either as a lump sum each semester or in 3 installments (with no additional interest for installments).
- The payment deadline for lump-sum semester payments for Spring is January 15-17, 2025.
- Installment payment dates are as follows:

Installment       Payment Date
1st installment   January 17, 2025
2nd installment   February 17, 2025
3rd installment   March 17, 2025

For lump-sum semester payments:
- Payments can be made at any Akbank branch via the Akbank Subscriber Collection System (ATS) to Sabancı University's account using company code 7000754, selecting the debt type, and entering the student number (000xxxxx).
- If you have an Akbank deposit account, you can also pay via Akbank Internet Banking under: Payments > Institution Payments > Education Payments > Sabancı University, using your student number (000xxxxx).
- Detailed payment instructions will be sent via email along with your tuition fee payment information.

For installment payments:
- Visit any Akbank branch and request a "School Family Account" (product code "601") from a Personal Customer Relations Officer. Payments are processed using your student number.
- Submit your installment payment request along with the program fee information to the bank officer.
- Once approved, the branch will assign a Plus Money Limit (credit limit) to the account equal to the tuition fee.
- The first installment must be deposited into the account by the due date. If not paid, Akbank will activate the Plus Money credit limit, and the account holder will be charged to complete the payment.
- Detailed payment steps will be sent via email with your tuition fee payment information.
