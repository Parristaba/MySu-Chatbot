You must make your official transcript requests using the Su-Ticket
E-signed transcripts are provided free of charge.
In order to receive an official transcript, all your financial obligations to Sabancı University must be completed.
You can also view your transcript via the Student Information System, where you log in with your username and password. After logging into the Student Information System, you can view your transcript in the 'Academic Transcript' section by clicking on the 'Student Records Information Menu' link under the 'Student & Financial Aid Menu' menu.
The unsigned and unsealed transcript obtained through the Student Information System is not an official document.
