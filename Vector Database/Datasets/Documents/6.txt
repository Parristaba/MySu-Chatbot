Request of Cancellation of Enrollment For Fundation Development Year Students

Students can submit a request for cancellation of enrollment by completing the online University Leaving Form via SU Form.

Procedures to be Followed to Cancel the Enrollment;

Students who apply for cancelling the enrolment, should not have any unpaid balance to the University and should return all the goods and equipment provided by SU for the student's use without any damage. (Details are explained in Instruction letter for SL Clause 20, Instruction letter for Undergraduate Programs Clause 32, Instruction letter for Graduate Programs Clause 39)

After contacting the his / her academic/thesis advisor and departments specified on the form, the student guarantees that he/she does not have any requirements to be fulfilled by the signatures of the related departments. Please take into consideration the following steps for withdrawal; 

 Information Technology: Make sure that you delivered your lap-top and other accessories. 

 Faculty/Institute: Remember to deliver any keys of laboratories, offices etc. 

 Operation and Technical Service: Dormitory room keys (if you were staying in the dormitories), should be delivered. 

 Information Center: Books, CD's and DVD's that are borrowed should be returned. 

 Accounting: Make sure that you do not have any financial requirements to the University.

Approval of his / her Advisor: Approval of the student's academic/thesis advisor
