Students who have studied in the Foundation Development Year (FDY) for at least one academic year are considered unsuccessful in the following cases:

Students who fail to submit a valid language exemption certificate on the dates determined by the University at the end of each semester while enrolled in FDY.
Students who fail to meet the success requirements determined by the University for the final level that must be completed while enrolled in FDY.
Students who fail to pass the English Language Assessment Exam (ELAE).
Rights of Students Who Are Unsuccessful in FDY:

After completing one academic year in FDY, students may either continue their FDY education for one or two additional semesters or apply for the "externally prepared student" status, where they continue improving their English language skills independently outside the university.

Students who wish to continue for one or two additional semesters in FDY must submit a petition to the School of Languages Directorate within the dates announced by the University and pay the required tuition fees for the relevant semester. Based on the evaluation of the School of Languages Board, students are placed into an appropriate level.

The period spent under the externally prepared student status is included in the total education period of FDY. These students can start their undergraduate programs if they pass the ELAE administered by the University or submit a valid language exemption certificate on the dates determined by the University.

Students who fail to be successful at the end of the two academic years in FDY will have their registration terminated and will be placed under the “academically unsuccessful student” status by the decision of the School of Languages Board. These students may continue to improve their English language skills independently outside the University.

Students whose registration has been terminated will be granted the right to take the ELAE three times within three years on the official exam dates determined by the University. Alternatively, they may submit a valid language exemption certificate to be evaluated within the same time frame.

To sit the exam, students must submit a written application to the Student Resources Directorate at least one month prior to the ELAE exam date.

There are no make-up exams for students who miss the ELAE.
