In undergraduate education, the provisions regarding the termination of enrollment by the University are applied as follows, in accordance with the relevant regulations and decisions of the Higher Education Council:

 

At the End of the Maximum Period of Study:
The normal duration of undergraduate diploma programs is four academic years (eight semesters); the maximum duration of study is seven academic years (fourteen semesters), excluding the foundation year and optional summer terms. At the end of this maximum duration, including the examination and/or additional time rights granted under Article 44/c of Law No. 2547, students who fail to meet the graduation requirements will have their enrollment terminated by the University.

 

At the End of the Not-Registration Period;
     a. In accordance with the decisions of the Executive Board of the Higher Education Council, international students who remain not-registered for two consecutive years will have their enrollment terminated by the University.

     b. According to Article 44, paragraph c of Law No. 2547, students who remain not-registered for four consecutive years will have their enrollment terminated by the University with the approval of the Higher Education Council.
