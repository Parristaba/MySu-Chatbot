General Information:

According to YÖK regulations, graduates of the Faculty of Engineering and Natural Sciences will have their title mentioned on their diplomas. However, graduates from other faculties will not have a title on their diplomas.

The names of graduates to be written on the diploma are based on the information provided in the following documents:

     For Turkish citizens, the name and surname on the national ID card.

     For international students, the name and surname on their passports.

 

In Case of Name Change:

After graduation, if a graduate changes their name and/or surname for any reason (court decision, marriage, divorce, etc.), a new diploma or temporary graduation certificate reflecting these changes will not be issued. However, if the graduate submits a written request along with the required official documents, a statement will be added on the back of the diploma stating that "the person whose name appears on the front of the diploma is the same as the person mentioned in the official document." The diploma will be sealed and returned to the graduate.

 

Diploma Distribution:

Diplomas are given to students who have earned their graduation rights and completed the necessary procedures to receive their diploma.

Diplomas will not be given to others. However, if the graduate submits a notarized letter of request to the Student Resources, the diploma can be delivered to the person mentioned in the letter after identity verification and signature.

 

Diploma Supplement:

The Diploma Supplement is a document designed to help with the recognition of diplomas and degrees by relevant institutions and organizations. It was developed by the European Commission, the Council of Europe, and UNESCO/CEPES to increase international transparency. The primary purpose of this document is to facilitate the recognition of academic and professional qualifications (diploma, degree, certificate, etc.).

The Diploma Supplement provides detailed information about the structure, level, content, and status of the graduate's successfully completed studies. This allows graduates to more easily access job, master's, and doctoral opportunities abroad.

The Diploma Supplement is a supplementary document to the diploma; it does not replace the diploma.
