Conditions of repeating a course is stated as below.

Students who get an 'F', 'U', 'NA' or 'W' grade for University Courses offered in first year of education ; must take the subject course again in the following first semester it is given.
Students who get an 'F', 'U', 'NA' or 'W' grade for elective or courses dropped from the curriculum, should take a substitute course approved by his/her advisor. 
For students who complete all courses offered in his/her diploma programs and other graduation  requirements;  this condition is not required.
Students may repeat the courses in which they received passing grades ( (A, A-, B+, B, B-, C+,C, C-, D+, D) within 3 semesters at maximum following the semester they have taken it. The periods of semester leave and summer semester are not included in the semester counts.
In the calculation of CGPA*, the previous point received for the repeated courses is replaced with the new point however all the registered courses of the student are shown in the transcript.

* CGPA is calculated with SU credits.
