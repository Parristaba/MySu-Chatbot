Midterm programming: 
 
Midterm exams of first grade university courses are programmed by the Foundation Development Directorate.
All other courses are organized by the faculty member. Classroom reservations required for the midterm exams of these courses are also made by the academic members through the "Event Management".
The date / time / class changes in the announced midterm program will be announced by the course instructor at least two weeks before the examination date, in the course and on the internal web.
The midterm exams are displayed on "mySu/SUcalendar/Mid-Term".
 
Final exam programming:

 
The exams of all our faculty members who wish to schedule the final exams by the SR are during the exam period started in the Academic Calendar.
Final examinations are prepared taking into account the students enrolled on the last working day of the second week following the deadline for the add-drop period for the relevant semester.
The final exam program is formed by taking care not to program more than one final exam on the same day.
The end-of-term exam length for each ourse is programmed as two and a half hours.
Final exams are programmed between 09:00-11:30, 11:50-14:20, 14:40-17:10, 17:30-20:00 by placing a total of four test sessions.
There is no final examination for a class discussion, seminar, problem solving or laboratory hours.
Prepared final exam program will be announced on the web five weeks before the final exam dates who started in the Academic Calendar.
The changes requested for the final exam program can be made at the latest four weeks before the exam date.
All changes, who is requested after the announcement of the final exam program, can only be made with the approval of the dean.
The faculty members are responsible for the announcement of the changes, who made by the dean approval within the university at least one month before the exam date.
Final exams can be viewed from “Information System/Final Program”.
