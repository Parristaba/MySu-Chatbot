One academic year in FDY consists of fall and spring semesters, each of which is 16 weeks. Students’ English level is determined according to English Language Assessment Exam (ELAE) at which level they start their education. For a successful completion of a level, students should fulfill the attendance requirements for a course and has to complete the level by successful performance at a given level, which is assessed through in-class work, mid-term examinations, final exams Maximum education period for FDY is two academic years. Maximum education period calculation is being done as of 2014-2015 Academic year according to Law 6569 published on official gazette numbered 29187.

You can find information about the Foundation Development Year (Preparatory Language Education) here.

 

Students who successflly complete FDY can start undergraduate or graduate programs after fulfilling the the conditions below:

• Undergraduate FDY students who successfully complete the last level by fulfilling the conditions determined by the University,

• Undergraduate FDY students who submit language proficiency exam within the dates stated in the Academic Calendar, (You can access detailed information about the exemption from FDY)

• Undergraduate students who become successful at ELAE after having one year education in FDY,

 . Graduate students who are sconsidered as successful  at the end of FDY with the evaluation of the Graduate Schools.

The below-mentioned conditions are valid for students, who cannot meet the required conditions stated above, after having one year education in FDY:

• Maximum education period is 2 academic years in FDY. Students, who are unsuccessful at the end of the first year, may continue their education one or two more semesters in FDY. These students should apply to School of Languages within the stated period of time along with a petition.

• Students who do not  want to attend FDY for one or two more additional semesters, should submit "Student Preparing from Outside Application" petition to SL Directorate by asking for additional time. These students are able to use their additional time to improve their English skills with their own means as "External Preparatory Student "; students under this condition are reguired to complete their obligation check towardas University. 

• These students earn right to start undergraduate or graduate programs if they are successful at ELAE ( if only they apply ) or if they provide a Language Exemption Certificate* within the stated period determined by the university.

• Semesters spent as "External Preparatory Student"  are taken are into account for total education period in FDY.

Students who become unsuccessful at the end of two academic years in FDY:

• Students are dismissed from the university in accordance with School of Languages Board decision because of academic failure. Students, who complete the maximum period of education, may  get prepared outside  for the exams by their own means. Students are given three ELAE exam rights to be used within three years and to submit an English Proficiency Certificate within the announced period of time by the University.

• Students who do not renew their registration in FDY or who do not apply for additional period for education become "Not -Registered" Students.  Students who do not make tution fee payment for four consecutive years and become "Not -Registered"  Students due to this, are dismissed from the University with the approval of YÖK ( Turkish Higher Education Council).

*Language Exemption Certificate: Certification showing that the students have passed one of the national and/or international foreign language examinations recognized by Sabancı University at a level determined by Sabancı University.
