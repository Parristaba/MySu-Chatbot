For New Students:
Your student ID card is an important document that not only proves you are a Sabancı University student but also allows you to access various services on campus. When new students complete their registration in the Student Information System, they automatically submit their ID card request by uploading their photo. The ID cards will be distributed by the Security Office starting from the orientation date.

 

Important Notes:

If you lose your card, you need to pay a processing fee of 150 TL to the Akbank Central Branch account number 9999 (IBAN: TR53 0004 6007 1388 8000 0099 99) for a new or temporary student ID card.
You can report your lost card by sending a ticket to the SER - Lost Student ID Card address along with your payment receipt, stating your student ID number.
Your new or temporary ID card will be ready within 2 business days after your request.
You can collect your card from the Security Office in Building D2 after your request.
For more information, please visit the Security website.
