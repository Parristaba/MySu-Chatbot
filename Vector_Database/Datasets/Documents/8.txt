Application for Semester Leave

To apply for a semester leave, the documentation of your application reason and a written request should be submitted to: To the Faculty Dean's office of the the student is enrolled at, until the last work day of the 4thweek after beginning of classes. Applications for semester leave after these dates are not evaluated.

Evaluation and Conclusion of the Semester Leave Application

The request for semester leave is reviewed and concluded following the approval by the Faculty Administrative Board. The date of the relevant Faculty Administrative Board's approval is taken as the beginning of the leave period. 

Student Rights in the Period of Semester Leave
Students right, benefit and any opportunity, in the period of semester leave, is determined by the university.

Procedures for the Semester leave process after the approval decision:

A student who applies for leave in their first academic year at the university and whose application is accepted must pay the full tuition fee for the semester if they are granted leave for one semester, or the full tuition fee for the academic year if they are granted leave for the entire academic year, in order for their leave to be processed. All students are required to pay based on the full tuition amount, without any scholarships or discounts.

To process the leave request of a student, who apply until the last business day of the 4th week following the first day of classes in the academic year following the year in which s/he registered to the university and if the request is accepted; student has to pay the 25% of the semester fee that s/he needs to pay, if s/he takes a leave for a semester; while it is required to pay 25% of the tuition fee that s/he needs to pay for the academic year, if the leave is taken for an academic year. In order to start leave procedure and to return the fees, students should not have any unpaid balance owing to the University. Tuition fees paid by students cannot be deducted from the fees due for future periods of study.

 
Important Notes to be Considered During Semester Leave

If the application for semester leave made by the student is accepted and all fees are paid by the student, the registered courses are deleted from the student's semester records.

Maksimum Period of Semester Leave

Undergraduate students can go on semester leave at most one academic year at once and for 4 semesters in total.

Procedures to be Followed Coming Back From Semester Leave

Following their period of semester leave, the students need to renew their registration after making the required payments according to their scholarships.
