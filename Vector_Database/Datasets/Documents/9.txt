A- Application Requirements

 Course withdrawal process is only for undergraduate students.

It can be done only for compulsory Fall and Spring terms, within the dates announced on Academic Calendar.

Maximum 2 course withdrawals can be done in a semester; while it is maximum 6 in total throughout the entire education period.

The course is graded with “W” if it is withdrawn. It is not included in the calculation of credit and grade point average.

Withdrawn course can be repeated.

If a course is repeated (including the courses graded with “W”), it is not possible to withdraw it.

 
B- Application Steps:

Students, who would like to withdraw from a course within the announced dates:

Are required to apply through ”Information System / Student & Financial Aid / Registration / Request to Withdrawal Form”

Applications are submitted to the approval of the course instructor online. Also, instructor receives an e-mail about each course withdrawal request, once it is submitted.

Instructor may “approve” or “reject” the course withdrawal request through the online system.

If the instructor approves course withdrawal request, system registration is created automatically to be processed; and, students can follow the process from the system.

Application approved by the instructor is processed.

Approved applications can be viewed on the transcript in the end of application process.
