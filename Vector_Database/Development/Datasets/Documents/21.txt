Undergraduate Scholarships:

 The application conditions for ‘Academic Achievement and Need Scholarships’ and ‘Monthly Cash Need Scholarship’ are announced within the University. Students who meet these conditions must apply on the announced dates.
To be considered for scholarship evaluations, students must upload the necessary application documents via the Student Information System and complete their online scholarship applications by the deadline.
Students who do not complete their applications after uploading their documents to the system on the application dates or who upload incomplete documents cannot be evaluated for scholarships. For this reason, it should be made sure that the application is completed in its entirety.
Scholarship Application Steps:

*Students Services&Financial Aid

*Financial Aid

*Scholarship And Financial Aid Applications

Application Form is created from My Applications Information.
Scholarship and Financial Aid Announcements (For monthly need scholarship and other special scholarship applications)
Actively published scholarship application announcements from Financial Aid & Other Scholarship Announcements are listed. The student applies to the active advertisement by clicking the Apply button at the end of the desired advertisement.
Documents Required for Application for Scholarships:

*Documents showing the income status of the mother and father;

If employed in a salaried job; salary or wage slips for the last month approved by the workplace 
If he/she is a merchant, industrialist, self-employed, or tradesman; a photocopy of the Tax Certificate of the last year
If he/she is a farmer; for those subject to exemption, a document approved by the mukhtar, for those not subject to exemption, a document approved by the Ministry of Agriculture or Chamber of Agriculture
If he/she is a pensioner, a salary status document approved by the Social Security Institution for the last month
If the mother and father are not working; a document from the Social Security Institution showing that they are not registered
If he/she is retired and working, a salary status certificate for the last month approved by the Social Security Institution and salary or wage slips for the last month approved by the workplace
*For those who live in rented accommodation; a photocopy of the lease contract and a current bank receipt showing that the rent has been paid

*If there is a sibling(s) studying at the university; Student Documents from their universities

*Document obtained through the e-government(E-devlet) account of the mother or father and containing the information of all family members ''Population Registration Sample Certificate Query ''

*For each of the mother, father, and student, the ‘Title Deed Information Inquiry’ document/screenshot obtained from e-government(E-devlet)

*For each of the mother, father, and student, the ‘Vehicle Registered in My Name Inquiry’ document/screenshot obtained from e-government(E-devlet)

Note: Documents with barcodes that can be obtained through e-government(E-devlet) are accepted. Document dates must be up to date.
