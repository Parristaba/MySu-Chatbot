The scholarships provided during the continuation of education due to academic achievement and/or need are evaluated by the Board of Trustees every year, and new quotas and opportunities can be determined.
 Scholarship evaluations are carried out once a year at the end of the Spring term of each academic year for the ‘Outstanding Academic Achievement’ and ‘Academic Achievement and Need’ scholarships, and twice a year in July and November for the ‘Monthly Cash Need Scholarship’.
‘Outstanding Academic Achievement’ and ‘Academic Achievement and Need’ scholarship evaluations are valid for partial scholarship and non-scholarship students. Students who are currently on full scholarship cannot be considered for these scholarship.
*Students are required to fulfill the following criteria for scholarship applications and evaluations;

*General criteria :

To have successfully completed at least two semesters (excluding periods of leave of absence) in the undergraduate program excluding TGY and to have passed to the second year,
To have completed at least 30 credits,
Not having applied for graduation for the end of the academic year in which the assessment is made,
Not to have been suspended in the last academic year in which the evaluation was made,
Students who have been reprimanded during the last academic year of the evaluation are at the discretion of the Scholarship Committee.
*Criteria for Scholarships for Outstanding Academic Achievement :

To have a GPA of at least 3.00 as of the end of the Spring term of the academic year in which the evaluation is made, and
Completion of at least 30 credits in the academic year of assessment, and
DNO must be at least 3.00 in the autumn or spring semester of the academic year in question, and
To have completed a maximum of 7 semesters of undergraduate education (except for students who started at the Basic level in TGY and continued as a TGY student in the autumn semester of the following academic year and whose maximum scholarship period has expired)
*Criteria for Academic Achievement and Need-based Scholarships:

To have a GPA of at least 2.50 as of the end of the Spring term of the academic year in which the evaluation is made, and
To apply within the announced deadlines to complete the necessary documents completely and on time, and
Being in real need to cover the costs of education,
*Allocation of scholarships awarded for ‘Outstanding Academic Achievement’;

Dilek Sabancı Scholarship: Students who have passed the 3rd grade among the students who meet the criteria are listed from high to low according to their GPA and the scholarship is allocated within the quota.

Sakıp Sabancı Incentive Scholarships; After the Dilek Sabancı Scholarship allocation is made, students who meet the above criteria are listed separately as 2nd Grade (34-63 credits), 3rd Grade (64-93 credits) and 4th Grade (94 and above credits). Each list is ranked from high to low according to their GPA. For each class, within the framework of the available resources, scholarship distributions are made in such a way that the scholarships of students without scholarships or whose current scholarship is below 50% are completed to 50%, and the scholarships of students whose current scholarship is above 50% are completed to 100%.

In the Case of Equal GPA : The following steps are taken into consideration in order to determine which student among the students with equal GPAs will be ranked higher in the list;

*More cumulative credits earned,

*Higher average academic year,

* More credits earned in the academic year.

Exchange Students : The actual academic achievement status of exchange students at the time of the evaluation is taken into consideration. For students whose grades are incomplete, the credit completed in the academic year is accepted as 30 and the DNO is accepted as 3.00 and the class in which these students will be evaluated is determined depending on this accepted completed credit.

Grade Changes : Grade changes made for any reason after the scholarship allocations are finalized do not change the scholarship lists.

*Allocation of scholarships awarded for ‘Academic Achievement and Need’;

In Case of Need Equality : Criteria such as higher GPA, more cumulative credits earned, higher academic year average, and more credits earned in the academic year play a positive role in the selection of the student who will be in the front row in the ranking of students with the same level of need.
