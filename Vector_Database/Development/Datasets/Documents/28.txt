Optional Summer School Application;
 
Education period: For each course, course hours are equal to Fall and Spring terms. Optional Summer semester is not included in the calculation of maximum education period.

Course load: For undergraduate students, maximum course load is 8 SU credits including the credits which will be taken from other universities. However, students, who will graduate after taking courses in  the Summer term, can increase their course load at most 10 SU credits  with the approval of their advisors and the decision of Faculty Administrative Board by submitting a written petition to the Faculty they enrolled at. Course load for graduate students is 9 SU credits.

Course Registrations : Course registrations can be done via Information System. Course registrations become definite after the fee payments. Course registrations will be invalid for the students who have not made payments on the announced payment dates.
