Students who have not yet declared a diploma program or who wish to request a change of diploma program are required to submit their applications within the dates specified below:

Application Dates: April 28 – May 9, 2025

Who Can Apply?

A. Major Declaration: Students who have completed the following courses and are at least in their second semester of study are eligible to declare their diploma program:

   - SPS 101

  - MATH 101

  - NS 101

  - HIST 191

  - TLL 101

  - AL 102 (ENG 101 for students admitted before the 2017–2018 academic year)

  - IF 100 (only for students admitted in the 2017–2018 academic year and later)

Students who enrolled prior to the 2020–2021 academic year and who have not completed the above courses may still apply, provided they meet the previously established credit-based requirements.

B. Diploma Program Change: Students who have already declared a diploma program and wish to transfer to a different one must apply through the internal transfer process.

 

Application Procedures

A. Major Declaration: Applications must be submitted through the Student Information System (Bannerweb) by following these steps:

Log into the Information System page.
Click on "Student & Financial Aid."
Proceed through the "Degree Audit and Graduation" section and select "Major Declaration for Undergraduate Students" to complete your application.
B. Diploma Program Change: Applications for a change of diploma program must be submitted online via the Student Petition Form and directed to the Dean’s Office of the relevant faculty.

 

Important Note for International Students


Applications for major declarations or program changes that involve a different type of admission score than the one originally used for university enrollment are considered within the scope of transfer procedures, in accordance with the regulations of the Council of Higher Education (YÖK).

In accordance with the relevant YÖK regulations and decisions:

   - International students must meet the admission criteria applicable to the target program group for the year of their initial enrollment. However, students who were admitted based solely on their high school GPA are excluded from internal transfer regulations and therefore are not eligible to apply for a diploma program with a different admission score type or to transfer to another program.

   - Students who are in their fifth semester or later and who apply to declare or change a diploma program involving a different admission score type will have their applications evaluated under the provisions of Additional Article 1 (EK Madde-1) concerning horizontal transfers. Since international students do not have a central placement score (ÖSYM score), it is not possible for them to declare or transfer to a diploma program with a different admission score type.
