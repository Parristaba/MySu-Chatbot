# Title: Application to Double Major and Minor Programs
# Date: Tuesday, April 29, 2025
-----------------------

Students who wish to enroll in a double major or minor program for the 2024–2025 Academic Year Summer Term must apply within the dates specified below.

Application Dates: April 28 – May 9, 2025

How to Apply?

It is made online with the Student Petition Form, which must be submitted to the Dean’s Office of the Faculty hosting the double major or minor program.

The application requirements must be fulfilled as of the beginning of the Summer Term when the evaluation will take place.


Important Note for International Students

For applications to FENS programs, in addition to other requirements, applicants must meet the condition of being ranked within the top 300,000 in the quantitative score type according to the YKS guide. Therefore, international students are not eligible to enroll in double major programs within FENS.

You can find detailed information on the Student Resources internal web page.
