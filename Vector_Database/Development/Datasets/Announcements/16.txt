# Title: About Suspension of Educational Activities
# Date: Wednesday, April 23, 2025
-----------------------

Dear Students,

Due to the earthquake that occurred in Istanbul on April 23, 2025, and in accordance with the decision taken by the Council of Higher Education (YÖK), all universities in Istanbul will suspend educational activities on April 24–25, 2025.

Within this scope, there will be no classes held at our University on the specified dates. Additionally, the exams that were scheduled to take place on our Tuzla Campus over the weekend (April 26–27) will be postponed to a later date to be determined by the respective course instructors.

Updates and information regarding new exam schedules and further developments will be communicated to you by the relevant academic departments.

We kindly present this for your information and wish you all good health.
