# Title: Graduation Application for 2024-2025 Spring and Summer Semesters
# Date: Thursday, April 17, 2025
-----------------------

All eligible students must submit a graduation application to ensure a smooth graduation process for the Spring and Summer Semesters of the 2024-2025 Academic Year. Failure to apply, even if all program requirements are met, will result in non-graduation.

Graduation Application Period: March 17, 2025 - May 05, 2025

Who Can Apply?

Undergraduate and graduate students set to fulfill program requirements by the end of the Spring and Summer Semesters are eligible.
Previously applied students anticipating completion of the program requirements in the 2024-2025 Academic Year Spring and Summer Semesters must reapply.
Application Procedure:

Graduation applications are submitted through the Student Information System (Bannerweb). Follow these steps:

Access the Information System page.
Click on "Student  & Financial Aid"
Navigate to "Degree Audit and Graduation" and complete the "Graduation Application Form" selecting the graduation period as 202402 - Spring 2024-2025 or 202403 - Summer 2024-2025.
Tracking Graduation:

Monitor your graduation progress via the Student Information System (Bannerweb) with the following steps:

Access the Information System  page.
Click on the "Student & Financial Aid" menu.
Select "Grade Audit & Graduation"
Click on "Degree Evaluation (Summary)"
Choose the graduation period: 202402 – Spring 2024-2025 or 202403 - Summer 2024-2025.
Click the "Generate New Request" button

Detailed information is available on the Student Resources internal web page
